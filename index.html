01 / Profile

About

I’m a postdoctoral researcher at Brigham and Women’s Hospital and Harvard Medical School, affiliated with the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard. I work on machine learning for clinical trials and decision support in cardiology, with an interest in data-driven personalised medicine.

I completed my PhD at Universitat Pompeu Fabra in Barcelona, where I developed methods for working with heterogeneous clinical data. My work has been supported by the Fundació Privada Daniel Bravo Andreu and has involved collaborations in cardiology and machine learning.

Outside research, I’m interested in creative coding, generative AI, music production, and the philosophy of science and ontology, especially Wilfrid Sellars and Gustavo Bueno.
